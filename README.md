# Cooling Without Power: Advanced Passive Design Strategies for Sustainable Home

## Project Overview

**Cooling Without Power** is a passive cooling design project focused on reducing indoor heat gain and maintaining comfortable indoor conditions without depending on conventional air-conditioning systems.

The project combines passive architectural and engineering principles such as **natural ventilation, thermal insulation, shading, reflective surfaces, and heat-resistant materials** to improve the thermal performance of a residential space while reducing energy consumption.

## Problem Statement

Conventional cooling systems consume significant electrical energy, especially in hot climatic conditions. Many residential buildings experience excessive heat gain through roofs, walls, windows, and direct solar radiation.

This project investigates how passive design strategies can reduce heat transfer and improve indoor thermal comfort with minimal or no electrical cooling.

## Objectives

- Reduce indoor heat gain using passive design methods.
- Improve natural ventilation and air circulation.
- Minimize dependence on electrical cooling systems.
- Improve thermal insulation of the building.
- Reduce overall energy consumption.
- Develop a practical and sustainable residential cooling concept.

## Key Passive Cooling Strategies

- **Natural Ventilation** – Uses strategically positioned openings to improve airflow.
- **Cross Ventilation** – Allows fresh air to enter from one side and hot air to exit through another.
- **Roof Insulation** – Reduces heat transfer from the roof into the interior.
- **Solar Shading** – Reduces direct solar radiation through overhangs, shading devices, and vegetation.
- **Reflective Surfaces** – Reflects solar radiation and reduces surface temperature.
- **Thermal Mass** – Helps absorb and delay heat transfer into the occupied space.
- **Ventilated Roof Design** – Allows accumulated hot air to escape from the roof region.
